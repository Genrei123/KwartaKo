## 1. Introduction
Kwarta Tracker is a personal finance mobile application built for the Filipino young professional who wants complete visibility and control over their money — across every wallet, every transaction, and every financial decision — without needing internet access or a bank integration.

Kwarta Tracker — App Proposal v2.0
Prepared by: Genrey O. Cristobal Platform: Flutter + SQLite (offline-first) Date: May 25, 2026

The app is built on one core philosophy: every peso should have a name. Not just tracked, but understood. When you log a ₱150 coffee, the app doesn't just subtract it from your balance — it tells you what percentage of your real free cash that is, which bucket it came from, and whether it delays your financial goals. When you log your ₱30,000 salary, the app automatically splits it into named buckets so the money is allocated before it can be spent accidentally.

### Kwarta Tracker has four pillars:
#### Cashbook
A running ledger of every peso in and out, across all wallets (GCash, Cash, BDO, Maya, or any custom wallet), starting from ₱0. The source of truth for your entire financial picture.
#### Impact Analysis
Every expense triggers a real-time analysis: Spending Pressure Index, Emergency Fund Erosion Rate, and Budget Bucket Cascade. The app tells you the real cost of a purchase before and after you confirm it.
#### Projection Engine 
Installment purchases, savings goals, and debt payoffs all have a calculated timeline. Decisions are made with full information, not gut feel.
#### Dashboards
Three dedicated views: Wallet Dashboard (where your money lives), User Dashboard (your financial health and goals), and Settings Dashboard (how the app behaves).
The app is fully offline. All data is stored locally on-device using SQLite via Flutter's drift package. No account required. No internet required. Your financial data never leaves your phone.

### 5. Formula Engine — Inputs & Expected Outputs
#### F1 — Allocation Engine (ZBB + Pay Yourself First)
```
Trigger: Every income entry
Inputs:
income_amount — amount being logged
active_installments — sum of all active monthly debt payments
emergency_ratio — from UserProfile (default 10%)
needs_ratio, wants_ratio, flex_ratio — from UserProfile
Formula:
emergency_contribution = income_amount × emergency_ratio
debt_deduction         = Σ(active_installments)
assignable             = income_amount − emergency_contribution − debt_deduction

needs   = assignable × needs_ratio
wants   = assignable × wants_ratio
flex    = assignable × flex_ratio

zero_check = income_amount − emergency − debt − needs − wants − flex
           → must equal 0
Expected output — example (₱30,000 salary, ₱4,500 debt):
Emergency contribution:  ₱3,000
Debt deduction:          ₱4,500
Assignable cash:         ₱22,500
  Needs:                 ₱13,500
  Wants:                 ₱4,500
  Flex:                  ₱4,500
Zero-sum check:          ₱0 ✅
```

#### F3 — Emergency Fund Erosion Rate (EFER)
```
Trigger: Every expense that causes free cash to go negative
Inputs:
expense_amount
remaining_free_cash — free cash left after prior expenses this month
monthly_emergency_contribution — from Allocation Engine
ef_current_balance — current emergency fund balance
ef_target — from UserProfile
Formula:
overspend        = max(0, expense_amount − remaining_free_cash)
daily_ef_rate    = monthly_emergency_contribution ÷ 30
days_delayed     = overspend ÷ daily_ef_rate

months_to_goal_before = (ef_target − ef_current) ÷ (monthly_emergency_contribution ÷ 1)
months_to_goal_after  = (ef_target − ef_current) ÷ max(0.01, monthly_emergency_contribution − (overspend ÷ 30))
Expected output — example (₱500 expense, ₱200 free cash left):
Overspend:              ₱300
Daily EF build rate:    ₱3.33/day
Days delayed:           90 days
Message: "This delays your emergency fund by 90 days.
          Completion moves from May 2028 → August 2028."
```

#### F4 — Budget Bucket Cascade (BBC)
```
Trigger: Every expense entry
Inputs:
expense_amount
expense_category — determines which bucket to hit first
wants_remaining, needs_remaining, emergency_remaining, flex_remaining — current month balances
Formula:
target_bucket    = category.defaultBucket

remaining = expense_amount
for each bucket in cascade order [target → needs → flex → emergency]:
    deducted = min(remaining, bucket_remaining)
    remaining = remaining − deducted
    if remaining == 0: break

cascade_depth = number of buckets touched

#### F5 — Financial Health Score
```
Trigger: Computed at end of each month, viewable anytime
Inputs:
monthly_debt_payments — total active installment payments
monthly_income — from UserProfile
ef_months — emergency fund balance ÷ monthly essential expenses
monthly_saved — amount contributed to savings this month
cash_flow — income minus all expenses
Formula:
dti_score      = 25 × (1 − (monthly_debt_payments ÷ monthly_income))
                 capped at 0–25

ef_score       = min(25, ef_months × 4.17)
                 (max score at 6 months of expenses covered)

savings_score  = min(25, (monthly_saved ÷ monthly_income) × 125)
                 (max score at 20% savings rate)

cf_score       = 25 if cash_flow > 0, else 0

health_score   = dti_score + ef_score + savings_score + cf_score
Expected output — Genrey, Month 1:
DTI (15%):       21.25 / 25
EF (0 months):   0 / 25
Savings (0%):    0 / 25
Cash Flow (+):   25 / 25
─────────────────────────
Total:           46 / 100  →  Grade: At Risk
Weakest point:   "Build your emergency fund. Even ₱500/month helps."
```

#### F6 — Installment Affordability Check
```
Trigger: SpayLater simulator
Inputs:
item_price, months
monthly_income, total_fixed_expenses, total_active_debts
ef_target, ef_current, monthly_emergency_contribution
Formula:
monthly_installment = item_price ÷ months
free_cash           = monthly_income − total_fixed_expenses − total_active_debts
buffer_after        = free_cash − monthly_installment
safety_buffer       = total_fixed_expenses × 0.10

verdict:
  buffer_after < 0               → Cannot Afford 🔴
  buffer_after < safety_buffer   → Tight 🟡
  buffer_after ≥ safety_buffer   → Safe 🟢

ef_monthly_adjusted = max(0, monthly_emergency_contribution − monthly_installment)
ef_delay_months     = (ef_target − ef_current) ÷ ef_monthly_adjusted
                      → "Infinity" if ef_monthly_adjusted = 0
```

#### F7 — Debt Priority Engine
```
Trigger: Debt Payoff screen
Inputs: List of debts, each with balance, interest_rate, minimum_payment, extra_payment
Formula:
Avalanche:
  Sort debts by interest_rate DESC
  Apply extra_payment to highest rate debt first
  Interest saved = Σ(balance × rate × months_eliminated)

Snowball:
  Sort debts by balance ASC
  Apply extra_payment to smallest balance first
  Months to first payoff = balance ÷ (min_payment + extra_payment)

For both: output total interest paid, total months to debt-free
```

#### F8 — Rule of 72
Trigger: Savings goal projector (investment mode)
```
Inputs: annual_return_rate (e.g. 6% for MP2)
Formula:
years_to_double = 72 ÷ annual_return_rate
Example:
MP2 at 6%  →  72 ÷ 6  =  12 years to double
